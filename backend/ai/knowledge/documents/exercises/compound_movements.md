# Compound Movements & Biomechanical Guidelines

## Fundamental Movement Patterns
1. **Horizontal Push**: Barbell Bench Press, Dumbbell Bench Press, Push-Ups.
   - Primary: Pectoralis Major, Anterior Deltoid, Triceps Brachii.
   - Cues: Retract and depress scapulae, 45-degree elbow tuck, maintain foot pressure.
2. **Vertical Push**: Overhead Barbell Press, Dumbbell Shoulder Press.
   - Primary: Anterior/Lateral Deltoid, Triceps, Upper Pectoralis.
3. **Horizontal Pull**: Barbell Bent-Over Row, Seated Cable Row, Dumbbell Row.
   - Primary: Latissimus Dorsi, Rhomboids, Middle/Lower Trapezius, Biceps.
4. **Vertical Pull**: Pull-Ups, Lat Pulldowns.
   - Primary: Latissimus Dorsi, Teres Major, Biceps.
5. **Squat (Knee-Dominant)**: Barbell Back Squat, Front Squat, Goblet Squat, Leg Press.
   - Primary: Quadriceps, Gluteus Maximus, Adductor Magnus.
6. **Hinge (Hip-Dominant)**: Romanian Deadlift (RDL), Conventional Deadlift, Hip Thrust.
   - Primary: Hamstrings, Gluteus Maximus, Erector Spinae.

## Safety & Injury Contraindications
- If athlete has knee pain: Regress heavy deep squats to Box Squats or Bulgarian Split Squats; prioritize Romanian Deadlifts and hamstring curls.
- If athlete has shoulder impingement: Replace straight barbell bench press with Neutral-Grip Dumbbell Press or Incline Dumbbell Press; avoid behind-the-neck pressing.
- If athlete has lower back pain: Swap conventional spinal-loaded deadlifts for Trap Bar Deadlifts or Chest-Supported Rows.
