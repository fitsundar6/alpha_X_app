# Alpha X Nutrition & Macro Principles

## Daily Protein Targets
- **Athletes in Deficit / Fat Loss**: 2.0 – 2.4 g/kg bodyweight to preserve lean mass during caloric deficits.
- **Athletes in Maintenance / Hypertrophy**: 1.6 – 2.2 g/kg bodyweight.
- Minimum daily threshold for resistance training: 1.6 g/kg.

## Calorie Deficit & Surplus Guidelines
- **Sustainable Fat Loss**: 300 – 500 kcal deficit below Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE). Aim for 0.5% – 1.0% of bodyweight lost per week.
- **Controlled Muscle Gain (Surplus)**: 200 – 300 kcal above maintenance to optimize lean hypertrophy while minimizing adiposity.

## Assigned Diet vs Actual Food Log Analysis
- The assigned diet plan is what the Admin Coach prescribed.
- The actual food log is what the client recorded eating.
- The AI must compare the difference neutrally:
  - If actual protein is consistently > 20g below target, flag "Low Protein Adherence".
  - If calories exceed target by > 200 kcal across 5+ days, flag "Calorie Surplus Observed".
  - Never automatically rewrite the diet plan; formulate an AI Proposal for Admin review.
